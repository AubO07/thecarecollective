The Problem Today

One Household. Two People. Fragmented Public Support.

Carers spend hundreds of hours managing separate public services—chasing updates between GP surgeries, acute hospital discharge wards, council social care, housing, and DWP benefit teams.

  • Repeated Storytelling: Re-explaining care histories to every new professional.
  • Delayed Discharge Friction: 49% of 14+ day acute delays link to care transfer gaps.
  • 1.1 Million Left Work: Carers forced out of employment in the past two years alone.

6-Month Local Demonstrator

Testing in Richmond & Wandsworth

A 20-household consent-based demonstrator (10 Richmond / 10 Wandsworth) designed to establish baselines, track change, record non-improvements, and evaluate fiscal outcomes.

20 Consenting Families
6 Months Test Duration
£4,992 Avg Annual Work Restored

Demonstrator Pillars

  • Early Gains: Retain/regain paid work hours for willing carers.
  • Next Opportunities: Coordinate rehab, skills training, and flexible work.
  • Complex Needs: Join health and housing decisions before crisis occurs.

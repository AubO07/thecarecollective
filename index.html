The proposition

Connect the decisions.
Improve the outcomes.

Health, social care, housing, benefits and community organisations make decisions about the same lives — often without a shared plan. Connecting the Dots proposes a simpler way to work together: consent, clear ownership, joined-up actions and measurable outcomes.

NCS
Proposed national service

Care. Connected.

A future National Care Service is a policy ambition, not yet an operating service in England. Connecting the Dots proposes a practical bridge worth testing.

01 / SEE

One shared picture.

Listen to both the carer and the person receiving support; bring relevant, permissioned information together without merging their identities.

02 / ACT

One coordinated plan.

Named owners, clear decisions and visible next steps across existing organisations, each retaining its own responsibilities.

03 / MEASURE

One test of value.

Check whether less repetition and better coordination genuinely release time, improve independence and expand opportunity.

The outcome that matters

From caring and being cared for, to living, learning and contributing.

Our ambition is to help carers and people receiving care regain the freedom to learn, train, develop and work — where that is what they want and where the right support makes it possible. A more connected system should give time and possibility back to people.

Start locally. Learn publicly.

The proposed demonstrator.

Begin in Richmond and Wandsworth. Bring together existing partners around consenting carers and people receiving care. Test how decisions can be connected without waiting for a whole new platform or bureaucracy.

The aim is a six-month, independently reviewed demonstration: agree the baseline, test the model and publish what actually changed.

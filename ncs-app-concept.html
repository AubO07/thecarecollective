The family sees one journey. Services see what they must do next.

This is a concept, not finished software. Its job is to make the operating principle tangible enough for carers, carees and professionals to challenge and improve together.

One shared picture

The person and carer can see the same current position as the services around them—without repeating their story at every door.

Named responsibility

Every next action has an accountable service and a visible status. A missed handover cannot quietly disappear.

Permission from people

The carer and caree control what can be shared, with whom and for what purpose. Consent is specific and visible.

Action before crisis

Delays and contradictions are surfaced early, when coordinated action can still protect recovery, independence, learning and work.

Co-production starts here.
This screen should be tested with carers, carees and frontline professionals before a technical specification is written.
